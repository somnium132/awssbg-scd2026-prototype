Event Details

The Blueprint

The theme runs through the whole day — three motions instead of one track: what you build, what powers it, and how you lead with it. The same agenda runs in parallel at both the Laguna and Batangas venues.

Agenda: Build. Power. Lead.

01

Build — hands-on with Cloud & AI

Beginner-friendly technical talks and demos on AWS cloud fundamentals and applied AI — made for first-timers, not just the already-initiated.

02

Power — community & representation

Women in Tech spotlights, student success stories, and career pathway sessions that power a more diverse, self-assured builder community.

03

Lead — from participant to leader

Panels, mentorship touchpoints, and community showcases that help students take the next step — organizing, mentoring, and leading their own chapters.

What is SCD?

A student-first stage for Cloud & AI

AWS Student Community Day (SCD) is a student-organized, AWS-supported gathering that brings cloud and AI closer to students who don't yet see themselves in tech. South Summit 2026 is the CALABARZON edition — built by student leaders, for students.

Instead of another highly-technical conference, this summit balances real technical exposure with human-centered experiences: beginner-friendly cloud & AI talks, Women in Tech empowerment sessions, career pathways, and a community hub built for networking — not just watching.

  • See the opportunity. Get a clearer picture of where Cloud and AI careers actually start.
  • Build real confidence. Leave with the belief that you belong in this industry.
  • Make real connections. With fellow students, organizers, and industry partners.
  • Leave with next steps. Practical, doable actions — not just inspiration.
